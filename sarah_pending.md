# Things Sarah found and is waiting on your OK for

Written by Sarah's unattended check-ins (see sarah_agent.py) — she reads your
connectors on her own every few hours and drafts a line here for anything
that looks like it needs a decision from you, instead of acting on it without
you around to approve it. Ask her "what's pending" and she'll walk you
through this. Once you approve something, she does it for real and updates
or removes the line.
